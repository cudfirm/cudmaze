How It Works

Shopping on CUDMAZE takes four simple steps, and you pay the way that suits you.

1. Find what you need

Browse by category, search for an item or visit a seller’s store. Every listing shows its price and photos.

2. Choose your options

Open the item, pick your size, colour or quantity and add an optional note, like a price request or a customisation.

3. Order on WhatsApp

Tap Buy on WhatsApp. A ready-written message with the item, price and link opens in WhatsApp. No account needed.

4. Confirm, pay & receive

We confirm availability and delivery fee, you pay, and a dispatch rider brings it to your door. Inspect before you accept.

Want a saved cart? Create a free account to keep items in your cart across devices and check out several items in one WhatsApp message.
